This is a simple and wordy site. When I don't have large current projects, I am adding more content, and when I do, I am scribbling notes to publish later.

Every article on my website has been thoroughly researched. The longer articles contain from 70 to 150 references, I read over 500 search results, and the longer texts can take a month each to write.
Hope you find the reading it adds value, I do not consider my articles to be a blog.

This site is a engineering workbook and aide-mémoire. To misquote the start of many thesis and dissertations, “Any / all the errors in this text are mine and made by me”. To date, there are no AI generated articles on here, as that would reduce learning. When I need to gain perspective on a category of tech, I need to achieve the broad overview of a listicle. However I am trying to avoid these since OpenAI got more main-stream.
My site has few pictures, as I do not have a process to communicate with them (and genAI makes this worse).
